## Page description

Grandmama left you her farm. It is one old hen, a bare field and a bicycle.

You are the hand that runs the company a raccoon founds on top of it — and he
is *right there*, in a top hat, walking his own land, taking the credit the
moment anything lands, and explaining at length that he is not anti-regulation,
he is pro-loophole.

Sweep eggs. Sell them. Buy seed. Feed the hens. Hatch better hens. Buy a cart,
then a van, then a route to a town that pays more. Twenty plots of valley, six
ages of the company from straw to Jurassic, 128 chicken species, a gene lab, a
kitchen, a chicken park, a fleet, a crew and eventually a seat on the market.

**Nothing in this game is a file.** No image files, no audio files, no icon
fonts, no emoji. Every sprite, every letter of both pixel typefaces, and every
note of the music is generated in code at runtime. The whole thing is one HTML
file.

### What's in it

🦝 **A founder with opinions.** Eleven expressions, eighteen poses, a wardrobe
of hats and glasses, and about a hundred lines of dialogue — on the weather, on
the ledger, on the hens' status as stakeholders who just do not know it yet.

🐔 **128 chicken species** across eleven tiers, with five genes each, a gene lab
that splices, clones and crosses them, and a rank ladder to push them up.

📰 **The Cluckton Chronicle.** Every few minutes the valley has an opinion about
you, and it arrives as a newspaper spinning in out of nothing. An egg inspector
wants a fee or an argument. The crew forms a committee. The council has noticed
the fence. An election is called and you can donate to both sides at once.
Twelve events, two ways out of each, and a standing decree afterwards that moves
what eggs are worth, what the crew costs and how busy the road gets.

🎺 **A band that plays itself.** A brassy, strutting, minor-key number for the
menu — written for this game — and something slower for the valley, both
synthesised live out of oscillators and one buffer of noise.

🦡 **No people anywhere.** Every worker, passer-by and day-tripper is another
species on the founder's own frame — fox, badger, possum, cat, otter, hare — or
a little hovering service droid.

🌲 **A valley that carries on without you.** Cars both ways, one in five pulling
over to gawp. Loose livestock on the verge. Wild birds crossing the sky in loose
Vs. Fireflies in the forest over the road. Worms and grubs in the soil that the
hens will chase down if you dig them up.

🎨 **A sandbox to paint.** The land starts bare — no pond, no paths, not a stone.
Every tree, terrace and pond on your ranch is one you put there, with brushes
that dither their own edges.

### Controls

Mouse or touch, that is the whole of it. Pick a tool from the rack on the right,
drag on the land, tap anything to inspect it. Works on a phone.

### Credits

Built with Claude Code. Everything procedural.

The menu theme is an original composition written for this game in the spirit of
a certain big-band villain number — the melody, the changes and the founder's
own boasts over the top of it are this game's own.
